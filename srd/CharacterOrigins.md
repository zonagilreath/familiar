# Character Origins — Species Flavor Reference

## Dragonborn
Humanoid, Medium (5–7 ft). Descended from a dragon progenitor — lineage determines elemental affinity (acid, lightning, fire, poison, cold). Can exhale a magical breath weapon. Gain spectral draconic wings at higher levels.

## Dwarf
Humanoid, Medium (4–5 ft). Resilient and stout. Naturally resistant to poison. Possess Stonecunning — a tremorsense ability when touching stone. Exceptionally tough, even by adventurer standards.

## Elf
Humanoid, Medium (5–6 ft). Graceful and long-lived. Resistant to charm effects. Three lineages: **Drow** (deep darkvision, dancing lights, darkness magic), **High Elf** (arcane aptitude, wizard cantrips), **Wood Elf** (swift, druidic, stealthy in nature). Enter a meditative trance instead of sleeping.

## Gnome
Humanoid, Small (3–4 ft). Clever and curious. Resistant to mental magic (Advantage on Int/Wis/Cha saves). Two lineages: **Forest Gnome** (illusion magic, speak with animals), **Rock Gnome** (tinkering, clockwork devices).

## Goliath
Humanoid, Medium (7–8 ft). Descended from giants. Powerful build — count as one size larger for carrying/grappling. Can grow to Large size at higher levels. Giant ancestry grants themed abilities: teleportation (cloud), fire, frost, knockdown (hill), endurance (stone), or thunder.

## Halfling
Humanoid, Small (2–3 ft). Brave (resistant to fear), nimble (can move through larger creatures' spaces), and lucky (can reroll natural 1s). Naturally stealthy — can hide behind larger creatures.

## Human
Humanoid, Medium (4–7 ft) or Small (2–4 ft). Resourceful and versatile. Gain extra skills and an origin feat. Naturally adaptable to any role.

## Orc
Humanoid, Medium (6–7 ft). Fierce and enduring. Can dash as a bonus action with an adrenaline rush. Deep darkvision (120 ft). Relentless endurance lets them shrug off a killing blow once per day.

## Tiefling
Humanoid, Medium (4–7 ft) or Small (3–4 ft). Bear a fiendish legacy — supernatural presence with thaumaturgy. Three legacies: **Abyssal** (poison-themed, venomous magic), **Chthonic** (necrotic-themed, deathly magic), **Infernal** (fire-themed, hellfire magic).
